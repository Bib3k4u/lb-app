Theme · ambient glass

Same room, better lighting

Three things carry the reference look, and all three are here. A warm bloom behind everything rather than a flat fill. Frosted cards — translucent white at about 5% with a backdrop blur, so each one picks up the colour behind it. And pill geometry on every action.

The result is depth without a single heavy drop shadow. Cards separate because they are made of different material, not because they have a border drawn round them.

The one decision left

  • Yellow is the default here. #F7D046 — golden rather than lemon, so it stays warm against the bloom instead of washing out. Brand orange is still one tap away above for comparison.
  • Yellow carries dark text, not white. White on this yellow is about 1.7:1 and genuinely unreadable; near-black is 12:1. The toggle moves --on-accent with the accent for that reason.
  • Your live site still ships #FF4400. Going yellow in the app means updating --primary there too, or accepting that the two look like different products.

Carried over

  • The streak ring is data. Seven arcs, one per day, filled when you trained and dimmed for today, still open.
  • One solid fill on the screen. Everything else is glass, so check-in reads as the action without needing to shout.
  • Real units. kg, reps, sets — volume in kilograms, not an invented score.
